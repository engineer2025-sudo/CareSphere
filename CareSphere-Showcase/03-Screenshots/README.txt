CareSphere — iPhone Blue-Frame Inside-Out Collection

122 full-resolution PNGs with transparent backgrounds.
Use 00-START-HERE.html to browse and filter, or the five 00-Index JPG sheets.
Screenshot-Index.csv lists views and languages. Manifest.json records hashes,
source commits, viewport, dimensions and frame provenance.

FRAME: The exact ORIGINAL blue/navy pitch-deck frame, using
make_assets.phone_screen_from_raw() and make_assets.phone_frame().
This is the same midnight-blue bezel and soft shadow now used in Video Deck.
The rejected silver/titanium collection frame is NOT used here.

CAPTURE: Real responsive WEB UI, 390 x 844 viewport captured at 3x.
The original frame renderer resizes the screen to 720px wide, and adds the
status area, home indicator, navy bezel and shadow outside the app content.
Final transparent PNGs are 1018 x 2000 pixels. These are NOT native iOS
screenshots. The phone status area is a drawn presentation element.
No AI-generated imagery or invented interface screens were used.

SOURCE: Running archived app a3d9e4489c935b67e4a6c55c133b02a2e639d6df.
Latest checked application head: 0af7c22518a7fa7d8caa3f795c9d0b022f7373a8.
The newer head changes capture/CI artifacts, not src/ or public/. The
captured UI is identical to that latest application's UI.

COVERAGE: Overview, mood, routines, medication setup, every activity type,
Solitaire states, Word Search themes, Bingo, picture matching/recall,
conversation and social scenarios, sensory controls, pause/hint/next states,
search/favorites/categories, Coffee Circles, Care Circle, visit preparation,
communication cards, visual routines, appointment questions, photo/contact
forms, interest prompts, offline references, research, encrypted transfer,
backup/recovery, notes draft/save/edit/delete review, unpaired vitals and
blank manual-entry forms, appearance/contrast/text size, Simple Mode, SOS
options, and language settings.

LANGUAGES: English; Simplified Chinese (10 views); Spanish (5);
Filipino/Tagalog (5); Vietnamese (5). Chinese screenshots use a proper CJK
font so characters do not turn into missing-glyph boxes. These are the
app's actual translations. Some advanced or external text remains English,
as the app itself explains. This gallery is not an independent linguistic
certification of every translation.

PRIVACY AND SAFETY: Names, contacts, medication details and health readings
were not populated. Example routines, appointment questions and notes are
clearly labeled "Example" and contain no patient data. They were entered
through real UI controls, not drawn onto screenshots. No calls/messages
were sent, no video room was joined, and no sensor connection was claimed.
Photo tools are shown empty to avoid private/personal photos. Native-only
Apple Intelligence, HealthKit and Android Health Connect cannot be shown
as functioning native features in a web capture and are not fabricated.

ATTRIBUTION: The app and its actual icon are from the repository. OpenMoji
artwork visible in activities is CC BY-SA 4.0, https://openmoji.org/.
The navy frame, visual index and HTML browser were drawn/written in code.
