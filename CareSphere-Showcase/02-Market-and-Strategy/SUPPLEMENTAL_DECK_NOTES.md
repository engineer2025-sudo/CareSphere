## Slide 7

Activation: completes a chosen routine/check-in, activity or visit-preparation action within seven days of first use. Retention: returns for a chosen useful action during days 61–90. 1,200 invites ×25%=300 activations; 300×40%=120 retained. Partners, rates and period are planning assumptions. Dedupe voluntary pilot participants; do not introduce mandatory accounts or hidden tracking. Retention measurement needs explicit consent and a defined method.
